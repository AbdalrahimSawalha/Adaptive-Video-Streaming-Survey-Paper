# Experimental Papers

This folder contains the experimental research papers reviewed as part of the literature review for the **Adaptive Video Streaming Survey Paper**.

The selected papers cover different experimental approaches to adaptive video streaming, including **machine learning and reinforcement learning, adaptive bitrate (ABR) techniques, congestion control, QoE optimization, bandwidth prediction, and edge computing**.

The papers were reviewed to identify and compare the approaches, techniques, and research directions relevant to the development of the survey.
