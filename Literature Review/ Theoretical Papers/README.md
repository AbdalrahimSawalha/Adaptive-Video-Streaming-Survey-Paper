# Theoretical Papers

This folder contains the theoretical and foundational references used to support the development of the survey paper on adaptive video streaming.

These papers provide theoretical concepts, principles, models, protocols, and research foundations relevant to areas such as congestion control, real-time video streaming, transmission mechanisms, network optimization, and perceived video quality.

## References

### [11] Test Cases for Evaluating Congestion Control for Interactive Real-Time Media

**Authors:** Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF)  
**Publication:** RFC 8867, January 2021

This reference provides standardized test cases and evaluation guidelines for congestion control mechanisms in interactive real-time media.

---

### [12] Deadline-Aware Transmission Control for Real-Time Video Streaming

**Authors:** F. Rossi et al.  
**Publication:** ACM CoNEXT, December 2022

This work addresses transmission control for real-time video streaming with consideration of packet delivery deadlines and time-sensitive communication requirements.

---

### [13] Combining Heuristic and Reinforcement Learning to Achieve the Low-Latency and High-Throughput Receiver-Side Congestion Control

**Authors:** Q. Zhang et al.  
**Publication:** IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking, August 2023

This paper presents an approach combining heuristic methods and reinforcement learning for receiver-side congestion control, targeting low latency and high throughput.

---

### [14] Improving Content-Aware Video Streaming in Congested Networks with In-Network Computing

**Authors:** G. Martinez et al.  
**Publication:** IEEE INFOCOM Workshops, May 2023

This work investigates content-aware video streaming in congested networks and explores the use of in-network computing to improve streaming performance.

---

### [15] Improving Perceived Quality of Live Adaptive Video Streaming

**Authors:** E. Kim et al.  
**Publication:** IEEE Transactions on Broadcasting, September 2023

This paper focuses on perceived video quality in live adaptive video streaming and examines factors affecting the user's viewing experience.

## Purpose

These references were used as theoretical and foundational material to support the understanding and development of concepts relevant to the survey, including:

- Congestion control
- Real-time video streaming
- Transmission control
- Network optimization
- Reinforcement learning for networking
- In-network computing
- Adaptive streaming
- Perceived video quality

The references in this folder complement the experimentally focused literature reviewed in the **Experimental Papers** section of the repository.
