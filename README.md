# Cross-Layer Intelligence for QoE-Centric Adaptive Streaming: AI-Driven Orchestration, Resilient Edge-NOMA Architectures, and Perceptual Latency Trade-Offs in 6G Networks

> **An Unpublished Academic Survey Paper**

## Overview

This repository documents the development of an academic survey paper focused on Adaptive Video Streaming, together with the supporting literature and research materials used throughout the study.

The survey explores how modern intelligent and network-aware techniques can be used to improve adaptive video streaming performance under dynamic network conditions.

The main research areas covered include:

- Adaptive Bitrate (ABR) Streaming
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Reinforcement Learning
- Congestion Control
- Buffer Management
- Quality of Experience (QoE) Optimization
- Edge Computing and Edge Caching
- NOMA-Based Architectures
- Low-Latency Video Streaming
- Network and Transmission Optimization
- Perceptual Quality and Latency
- Emerging 6G Networking Environments

---

## Research Objective

The main objective of this survey is to examine and organize existing research addressing the challenges of adaptive video streaming in increasingly dynamic and heterogeneous network environments.

Particular attention is given to the relationship between:

```text
Network Conditions
        ↓
Intelligent Adaptation
        ↓
Transmission & Resource Management
        ↓
Streaming Performance
        ↓
Quality of Experience (QoE)
```

The survey brings together research from multiple technical directions to examine how intelligent decision-making, network-level mechanisms, and edge-based architectures can contribute to improved streaming performance and user-perceived quality.

## Research Themes

The literature reviewed in this survey spans several interconnected research directions.

### Artificial Intelligence

Machine Learning and Reinforcement Learning approaches for intelligent bitrate selection, network prediction, adaptation, and congestion control.

### Adaptive Bitrate Streaming

Techniques for dynamically selecting video representations according to changing network and playback conditions.

### Congestion Control

Mechanisms designed to regulate network traffic and improve throughput, latency, and stability during video delivery.

### Quality of Experience

Approaches that optimize user-perceived streaming quality by considering factors such as bitrate, buffering, latency, and adaptation behavior.

### Edge Computing and Edge Caching

Architectures that move computation and content closer to users in order to reduce latency and improve resource utilization.

### NOMA-Based Streaming

Research exploring adaptive video streaming in multi-user wireless environments using NOMA and edge caching architectures.

### Low-Latency Streaming

Techniques targeting reduced end-to-end latency while maintaining high throughput and acceptable perceptual quality.

### 6G-Oriented Networking

Emerging networking approaches relevant to intelligent, highly adaptive, and heterogeneous communication environments envisioned for next-generation 6G systems.
